IPTV on TV and phone together: in practice (2026)

✍️ Isabella Reynolds · 📅 October 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read · 🛡️ Verified Review

Yes, you can watch IPTV on your TV and phone at the same time with a single premium subscription. This tutorial shows you exactly how to set it up using SubTV IP.

You only need the right credentials, supported apps, and a quick network tweak.

What you need

  • A premium subscription from SubTV IP (subtvip.store) – choose 1, 3, or 12 months. Activation is instant after payment.
  • Supported player apps: TiviMate 5.x, IBO Player Pro, IPTV Smarters Pro, Kodi 21, or Flex IPTV.
  • Compatible device for the TV: Fire TV Stick 4K Max, Samsung Smart TV (Tizen), LG webOS, Android TV Box, Xiaomi Mi Box S, or Apple TV.
  • A separate smartphone (Android or iOS) that can run the same or a different player app.
  • Stable internet (Wi‑Fi or Ethernet) – at least 25 Mbps for 4K streams.
  • Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1) to prevent ISP throttling, and a buffer/cache setting of 5,000 ms.

Step-by-step setup

  1. Get your subscription and login details. Go to subtvip.store, pick a plan, and pay via PayPal or credit card. After payment you receive an M3U URL or XTREAM codes (username, password, server). Save them.
  2. Install a player on your TV. Open the app store on your TV or streaming box. Search for “TiviMate” (or your preferred app from the list above) and install it. If you use a Fire TV Stick, use the Amazon Appstore.
  3. Enter your login credentials on the TV. Open the player app. Go to “Settings > Playlist > Add Playlist”. Choose the option for XTREAM codes or M3U URL. Type in your username, password, and server (or paste the M3U link). The app will load the channels.
  4. Set buffer to 5,000 ms. This prevents freezing. In the same player app, go to “Settings > Playlist > Buffer size” and select 5,000 ms. If your app does not offer this, go to its general playback settings.
  5. Install a player on your phone. On your smartphone, open the official app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). Search for “IPTV Smarters Pro” (or another supported player) and install it.
  6. Enter the same login details on your phone. Open the app. Choose “Login with XTREAM codes” or “Add M3U URL”. Type the same username, password, and server (or paste the M3U link). The phone will load the channels separately from the TV.
  7. Change DNS to Cloudflare 1.1.1.1. This reduces buffering caused by your internet provider. On a Fire TV Stick, go to “Settings > Network > Your Wi‑Fi network > Advanced > IP settings > Static” and enter DNS 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1. On a Samsung TV, go to “Settings > General > Network > Network Status > IP Settings > DNS Setting > Enter manually”. On your phone, you can set DNS in the Wi‑Fi settings or install the 1.1.1.1 app from the 9to5Google-covered Play Store.
  8. Test both devices at the same time. Open a channel on your TV and another channel on your phone. They should both play smoothly. If one lags, check your internet speed and verify the buffer setting.

If a step fails

  • Channels won’t load: Re‑check your login details. Make sure you copied the full M3U link or the correct XTREAM codes. Contact SubTV IP support via WhatsApp for help.
  • Buffering on one device: Confirm the DNS is set to 1.1.1.1 on that device. Also set buffer to 5,000 ms in the player’s settings. Reboot your router if needed.
  • App not available: Download a different player from the supported list. All of them work with the same credentials. For example, How‑To Geek often recommends IPTV Smarters Pro for beginners.
  • No picture or sound: Check that your TV supports HEVC codec (4K / 60 fps streams). All devices listed in “What you need” are compatible.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watch IPTV on TV and phone at the same time?

Yes. One premium subscription from SubTV IP allows you to stream on multiple devices at the same time. There is no restriction on simultaneous connections as long as you use the same login.

Do I need two subscriptions for TV and phone?

No. A single paid subscription works on both devices simultaneously. You do not need to buy a separate plan.

What is the best player for TV?

TiviMate 5.x is widely used for Android TV boxes and Fire TV Sticks. It has a clean interface and supports buffer adjustment. IBO Player Pro and IPTV Smarters Pro also run well on smart TVs.

How do I avoid buffering?

Set your player’s buffer/cache to 5,000 ms and use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1). Make sure your internet speed is at least 25 Mbps for 4K streams. Restart your router if you see frequent pauses.

Is there an instant activation?

No. SubTV IP does not offer a free trial or test period. You must purchase a premium subscription (1, 3, or 12 months) to get instant activation.

How do I contact support?

SubTV IP provides support via WhatsApp. You can find the WhatsApp number on their website after ordering or in the confirmation email.

Can I use a different player on my phone than on my TV?

Yes. For example, you can use TiviMate on your Fire TV Stick and IPTV Smarters Pro on your phone. Just enter the same login details in each app.
